Cane Rosso Menu & Prices: Dallas Pizza Guide 2026

Cane Rosso is a wood-fired Neapolitan pizzeria that started in Deep Ellum, Dallas, and now has five locations across the Dallas-Fort Worth area plus Houston and Austin. It is famous for the Honey Bastard pizza, and its pies are cooked in a 900°F wood-burning oven in under 90 seconds.

The official site does not publish dine-in prices, so the prices in this guide come from current DoorDash delivery listings — delivery prices typically run higher than what you pay at the table. Pizzas run $20.40–$25.20 on delivery: the Margherita is $20.40 and the signature Honey Bastard is $25.20. Pastas are $20.40–$21.60, the Whole Gem Caesar is $15.60, and OpenTable lists the restaurant at $30 and under per person.

Owner Jay Jerrier fell in love with real Neapolitan pizza on a honeymoon in Italy in 1995, trained with AVPN Master Pizzaiolos, ran a mobile catering oven from 2009, and opened the first Cane Rosso in Deep Ellum in early 2011. D Magazine named it Best Pizza in Dallas five years running, from 2011 to 2015. The original restaurant is at 2612 Commerce St, Dallas; reservations are accepted, catering is available, and the patios are famously dog-friendly. Gluten-free options are listed and vegan cheese is available. Guide last updated November 12, 2026.

Key facts

  • Honey Bastard — $25.20 (DoorDash delivery): hot soppressata, bacon marmalade, habanero honey and mozzarella; the most-ordered item.
  • Delivery pizzas $20.40–$25.20; Margherita $20.40. The official site publishes no dine-in prices — delivery runs higher than dine-in.
  • Pies cook in under 90 seconds in a 900°F wood-burning oven; dough from Italian “00” flour, sauce from hand-crushed San Marzano tomatoes, mozzarella pulled in-house.
  • Five DFW locations plus Houston and Austin; the original is at 2612 Commerce St, Deep Ellum, Dallas.
  • D Magazine named Cane Rosso Best Pizza in Dallas five years running (2011–2015); OpenTable lists $30 and under per person.
