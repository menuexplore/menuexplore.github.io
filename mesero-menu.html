Mesero Menu & Prices: What to Order at Dallas's Tex-Mex Favorite

Mesero is a Dallas-area Tex-Mex group, part of M Crowd Restaurant Group, that calls itself "beyond Tex Mex" — "Mexican Spirit, American Life." With nine locations across three states, it has built its reputation on slow-roasted brisket tacos, the award-winning Queso Mesero, bacon-wrapped camaron, the Rose Burger, and margaritas diners rate among the best in Dallas. Dallas-area locations include Victory Park, Inwood Village, Preston Hollow Village and Belt Line.

Tacos are the heart of the menu, with around ten styles listed. On current delivery menus, tacos run $17.49–$19.83, with the slow-roasted brisket taco — oven-roasted brisket with tomatillo sauce, queso chihuahua, onion, cilantro, mesero slaw and arroz rojo — at $18.66. The salmon and seared ahi tuna tacos are the lighter, seafood-leaning picks, while the quinoa taco gives vegetarian diners a dedicated option.

Starters are led by the award-winning Queso Mesero from $9.95, alongside queso tejano, guacamole from $9.95 and nachos from $11.95. Combinations #1–#6, all around $18.66, bundle favorites into one plate, and entrees stretch from fajitas from $18.95 to ribeye fajitas and ribeye around $45.30. The Rose Burger, a double meat, double cheese burger with stacked iceberg, tomato and pickle, is $17.70 on delivery menus. Margaritas and cocktails run about $14–$19.80, with the Original Rocks or Frozen at around $14.05 and the Jalapena Margarita and Prickly Pear Paloma at around $18.65.

The Victory Park location is open Monday–Thursday 11:00am–9:30pm, Friday 11:00am–10:30pm, Saturday 10:00am–10:30pm and Sunday 10:00am–9:00pm, and takes reservations.

Prices are based primarily on the restaurant's current online delivery menus (merchant-set, per DoorDash) — delivery prices often differ from dine-in, so confirm the latest menu before visiting. Guide last updated November 12, 2026.
