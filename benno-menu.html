Benno Menu: Breakfast, Dinner, Drinks & Prices

Benno is the beachfront restaurant at Hotel San Cristóbal in Todos Santos, Baja California Sur, Mexico, near Playa Punta Lobos. Its cooking combines Mexican flavours with Mediterranean-influenced techniques and focuses heavily on Baja California ingredients. Dinner is seafood-led — crudo, oysters, locally caught fish and house-made pasta — while breakfast covers Mexican-style dishes such as chilaquiles and breakfast burritos. Prices are in Mexican pesos (MXN), and the figures here are a recent menu snapshot rather than a permanent price list, because the menu changes with seasonal ingredients and the day's catch.

Verified dinner starters run MX$380–448: Crudo Benno, made with the catch of the day, is MX$448, grilled fish collars are MX$380, and roasted corn ribs and Benno hummus are MX$387 each. The Basque-style dark chocolate cheesecake is MX$310. Eight special cocktails are listed at MX$360 each, including Sol de Oaxaca, Verde Desierto and the Mexican Old Fashioned, and agave spirits are reported in the MX$350–520 range. OpenTable classes Benno at MX$500 or more per person, so that is a sensible starting budget.

Breakfast is served daily, but a complete current à la carte breakfast price list could not be independently verified — the one priced breakfast is the seasonal After Hike Breakfast at MX$450 per person, which includes coffee, a fresh juice shot, a house pastry and a choice of breakfast burrito or chilaquiles coloraditos. Benno is listed on OpenTable, dress is smart casual, and vegan and gluten-free options may be available — confirm dietary details and current hours with the restaurant before visiting.

  • Location: beachfront restaurant at Hotel San Cristóbal on Carretera Federal 19 near Playa Punta Lobos, Todos Santos, Baja California Sur, Mexico.
