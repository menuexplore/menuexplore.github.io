Trattoria Milanese Menu: Prices, Dishes & What to Order

Trattoria Milanese is a family-run restaurant at Via Santa Marta 11, 20123 Milan, in the Cinque Vie area behind Piazza Affari and within walking distance of the Duomo. It is known for traditional Milanese and Lombard cuisine, with reported specialties including risotto alla milanese, cotoletta alla milanese, ossobuco and mondeghili.

No complete dated official 2026 menu could be verified for this restaurant, so the dishes below are reported examples from restaurant coverage rather than a definitive current menu. The clearest detailed prices available come from a 2014 review — they are historical reference prices, not current 2026 prices. The guide's advice is to confirm current dish prices, side charges and any cover charge with the restaurant before ordering.

For a traditional Milanese meal, the guide suggests asking about risotto alla milanese with ossobuco — creamy saffron risotto paired with slowly braised veal shank. If you want Milan's famous cutlet, ask about the cotoletta alla milanese, a breaded veal cutlet traditionally cooked in butter. For a traditional starter, mondeghili — Milanese meatballs — are the most distinctly local option, alongside hot porcini mushrooms and artichokes with taleggio reported in a June 2026 review. Third-party listings report lunch around 12:00–14:30 and dinner around 19:00–22:30, but opening days vary between listings, so confirm hours before travelling.

Key facts

  • Address: Via Santa Marta 11, 20123 Milan, Italy (Cinque Vie, behind Piazza Affari).
  • Historical 2014 prices only: risotto alla milanese €11, cotoletta alla milanese €19, hot porcini mushrooms €14, potatoes €4 (priced separately from the cotoletta).
  • Signature dishes: risotto alla milanese, cotoletta alla milanese, ossobuco and mondeghili.
  • No current 2026 itemized prices could be verified — confirm prices with the restaurant before your visit.
  • Reported hours (third-party, unconfirmed): lunch 12:00–14:30, dinner 19:00–22:30.
