Shukette Menu & Prices 2026: What to Order in Chelsea, NYC

Shukette is chef Ayesha Nurdjaja's Levantine restaurant at 230 9th Avenue in Chelsea, New York: shared plates of house breads and dips, seasonal dishes from the Shuk, and proteins grilled over natural hardwood charcoal. The menu lists no prices online, so check today's prices in the restaurant. Shukette opened in 2021 and landed on The New York Times' list of the best new restaurants of that year; Nurdjaja has since been named a James Beard Foundation semifinalist for Best Chef: New York State. It is the younger sibling of Shuka, her SoHo restaurant (opened 2017), and part of The Bowery Group.

There is no appetizer-then-entree script here — the menu is a set of shared plates that arrive as they are ready, and the table builds the meal together. Bread is the tool the whole meal is eaten with: the signature "rip and dip" style means warm house-made breads, torn by hand, are used to scoop dips and sauces. Start with Frena, the Moroccan-style bread finished with garlic and oregano, or Lafa, grilled flatbread brushed with ramp butter and sea salt, plus Not Your Average Hummus, the most requested dip. From the Shuk, the seasonal vegetable-forward core, try the Glazed Sweet Potatoes or Pea Party in spring and early summer. From Al Ha'esh — "from the fire" — the Joojeh Chicken, a saffron-yogurt-marinated grilled half chicken served with toum, feeds two comfortably. Finish with The Mic Drop, tahini soft serve topped with halva floss, chopped hazelnut and pomegranate seeds; most tables split one between two or three people. The room is deliberately high-energy: a 23-seat concrete counter wraps the open kitchen, and the noise is part of the design. Guide last updated November 12, 2026.

Key facts

  • No prices listed online: Shukette's menu does not list prices online and dishes change with the seasons — check the restaurant's current menu or ask your server for today's prices.
  • Address: 230 9th Ave, Chelsea, New York, NY 10001. Phone: (212) 242-1803. Instagram: @shukettenyc.
  • Hours: dinner only — Monday to Saturday 5–11pm, Sunday 4–10pm.
  • Reservations: through Resy; a portion of the 23-seat counter is held for walk-ins.
  • Signature dishes: Frena bread, Not Your Average Hummus, Joojeh Chicken (feeds two), Jeweled Rice, and The Mic Drop tahini soft serve dessert.
