Durant's Menu & Prices Phoenix: Steaks $59–$185

Durant's is a classic 1950s steakhouse at 2611 North Central Avenue in Phoenix, founded in 1950 by Jack Durant, a former minor-league baseball player and Las Vegas pit boss. It is known for hand-cut steaks, slow-roasted prime rib, oversized martinis, red-flocked walls and crimson booths — and for its famous back-door entrance: guests walk up to an unmarked rear entrance and pass directly through the working kitchen on red floor mats.

Prices in this guide come from the restaurant's current official online menu. Steaks run $59–$185: the Famous Porterhouse is $175, the Wagyu NY Strip (14oz) is $185, and the Prime Rib is $65. Sides are priced individually at $16–$24 each, desserts run $13–$19, and the Signature Martini is $42. A classic dinner of a mid-range steak, a side, a dessert and a martini runs roughly $110–$140 per person before tax and tip.

Lunch is served Friday only, 11:00 AM–3:30 PM. Dinner hours are Monday–Thursday 4:00 PM–9:15 PM, Friday 3:30 PM–10:00 PM, and Saturday–Sunday 4:00 PM–10:00 PM. Reservations are strongly recommended. There is a dress code: athletic wear, printed t-shirts, sweatshirts, hats and visors are not permitted; collared shirts and sports coats are preferred. Guide last updated November 12, 2026.
