The Seafood Shack Menu & Prices 2026: Southend Fish & Chips Guide

The Seafood Shack is a seafood bar and grill on Eastern Esplanade in Southend-on-Sea, serving oysters, fish and chips, mussels, burgers and vegan options. The menu goes well beyond traditional fish and chips: alongside oysters, prawns, mussels, haddock and chips and seafood salads, it covers burgers, steaks, vegetarian and vegan dishes, desserts and drinks, plus rotating specials and a midweek set menu. Its online store also offers seafood platters and pre-order options.

Prices in this guide come from the restaurant's current online menu, and the guide was last updated November 12, 2026. Haddock & Chips costs £19, oysters start at £3.50 each, and the midweek set menu offers two courses for £18 or three for £23. Other mains include Scampi, Chips & Peas at £19, ½kg Fresh Mussels at £19 and Surf & Turf at £27, while the Wagyu Surf & Turf Burger is £21.50, the Fish Burger £18.50 and the Vegan Burger £19.50.

Lighter choices include the Crab Salad at £16, the Seafood Salad at £19 and the King Prawn & Crayfish Salad at £17. Desserts run £5–£8, including vanilla cheesecake at £8 and lemon drizzle at £7, and soft drinks start at around £2.50, with tea at £2.50 and coffee from £3 to £3.50. The set menu runs Wednesday and Thursday from 12pm to 8pm and Friday from 12pm to 4pm. The restaurant is at 32–35 Eastern Esplanade, Southend-on-Sea SS1 2ES, phone 01702 668394. Seasonal seafood and specials can change, so check the latest menu before visiting.

Key facts

  • Haddock & Chips £19; Scampi, Chips & Peas £19; ½kg Fresh Mussels £19; Surf & Turf £27 — current online menu.
  • Oysters £3.50–£3.95 each: plain £3.50; dressed, Bloody Mary and Oyster Rockefeller £3.95 each.
  • Burgers: Wagyu Surf & Turf Burger £21.50; Fish Burger £18.50; Vegan Burger £19.50.
  • Midweek set menu: 2 courses £18 / 3 courses £23 — Wednesday and Thursday 12pm–8pm, Friday 12pm–4pm.
