Sixty Vines Menu: Prices, Dishes, Brunch & Wine Guide

The Sixty Vines menu combines wine-country-inspired food with a large wine program, offering shared plates, salads, wood-fired pizzas, fresh pastas, steaks, seafood, desserts and cocktails. The restaurant's signature wine program features 60 wines on tap, while non-alcoholic beverages and coffee provide additional choices.

Because Sixty Vines operates multiple locations, its menu is not identical everywhere. Prices, individual dishes, brunch availability and hours can vary by restaurant. This guide focuses on the menu categories, sample Rice Village prices, meal-period differences, dietary choices, wine and drinks, and what to consider when choosing your order.

The food menu is organized around dishes that can work individually or be shared. Diners can find salumi and cheese boards, shared plates, salads, pizza, pasta, burgers, sandwiches, seafood, steak, desserts and brunch dishes. Pizza is one of the central categories — the Rice Village menu includes Margherita, Fig & Prosciutto, Shrimp & Smoked Bacon, Butcher's Pizza, Mediterranean Artichoke and a Gluten Free Mushroom pizza on a cauliflower crust.

Dinner generally provides the broadest selection, while lunch (Monday to Friday, 11 a.m. to 4 p.m.) adds sandwiches and soup, and brunch runs Saturday and Sunday from 10 a.m. to 3 p.m. All prices below are a Rice Village, Texas snapshot reviewed on October 2, 2026 — not a chain-wide price list — so check the menu for the specific location you plan to visit.

Key facts

  • 60 wines on tap — reds, whites, rosés and sparkling, rotating with the seasons and harvest.
  • Rice Village price snapshot reviewed October 2, 2026; prices vary by location — there is no single national price list.
  • Pizza: Margherita $14, Butcher's Pizza $17, Gluten Free Mushroom $20; gluten-free crust substitution for selected pizzas $3 extra.
  • Mains: Filet Mignon $41, Prime Steak Frites $36, Atlantic Salmon $27, Pork Chop $23.
  • Brunch Saturday–Sunday 10 a.m. to 3 p.m.; brunch entrées $16–$22; Mimosa $8.
  • Lunch Monday–Friday 11 a.m. to 4 p.m.; the Duo is $15 (half a pizza or soup with a half House or Caesar salad).
  • Desserts $8 each, including Basque Cheesecake; Tequila Fix cocktail $12.50.
