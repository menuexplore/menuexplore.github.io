Mi Cocina Menu & Prices (2026) – Tulsa & Flower Mound

Mi Cocina is a Dallas-born Tex-Mex restaurant group with locations across Dallas–Fort Worth, Tulsa, Oklahoma, and the Houston area, known for brisket tacos, sizzling fajitas, Sunset Enchiladas and the Mambo Taxi frozen margarita. Its official site publishes no item prices, so the prices in this guide come from third-party listings checked in October 2026 — delivery platforms (DoorDash, where delivery prices typically run higher than dine-in) and menu aggregators. Treat them as a current guide, not a permanent price list.

The menu centres on tacos ($16.75–$47.95), enchiladas ($17.10–$19.75) and fajitas ($17.45–$25.15+). Signature dishes include Tacos de Brisket ($21.90–$23.95), Sunset Enchiladas ($17.95–$18.55) and Sunset Style Fajitas (from $25.15). Starters run $9.55–$23.95, with chile con queso, queso blanco and Sunset Queso all listed from $10.15, and the Cocina Platter at $22.75–$23.95.

The Mambo Taxi is the drink that defines Mi Cocina: a frozen margarita of Sauza Silver tequila and fresh lime juice with a swirl of housemade sangria, described by the company as a Dallas original that accounts for more than 90% of its liquor sales. To-go listings put a single at $14.40 and a quart at $40.80. Weekday lunch specials run Monday–Friday, 11am–4pm ($7.95–$12.95), catering Mico Packs feed four for $52–$65, and the official site lists a full gluten-free menu plus vegan and vegetarian options.

All prices are third-party listings checked in October 2026; the official site publishes no item prices, and delivery prices typically run higher than dine-in. Guide last updated November 14, 2026.
