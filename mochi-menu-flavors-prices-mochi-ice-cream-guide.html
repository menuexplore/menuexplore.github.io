Mochi Menu: Flavors, Prices & Mochi Ice Cream Guide

Looking for a mochi menu with popular flavors, types, and pricing information? Whether you want chewy daifuku, creamy mochi ice cream, fresh fruit mochi, or trendy mochi donuts, this guide helps you understand what to expect before you order. Popular flavors include strawberry, matcha, mango, chocolate, ube, black sesame, and coconut, and most menus organise mochi by type, filling or flavor, serving size, and price.

The main types are mochi ice cream — a small portion of ice cream wrapped in a thin layer of chewy mochi dough, served frozen and often sold in multi-piece boxes or assorted packs; daifuku — traditional filled mochi with red bean paste, fruit, custard, or cream, served unfrozen; fruit mochi; and mochi donuts, a modern donut-style dessert with a chewy texture, coated with glaze, chocolate, matcha, or ube.

Prices vary by shop, location, type, and serving size, so the guide advises comparing the price per piece rather than the headline price alone — a menu may list a price per piece, a 4-piece selection, a 6-piece box, an assorted flavor box, or a gift box. Seasonal and specialty flavors may cost more, and flavors can change seasonally, so check the current menu of the specific shop before ordering.

Key facts & prices

  • Mochi ice cream: ice cream wrapped in chewy mochi dough, served frozen; popular flavors include strawberry, mango, matcha, chocolate, vanilla, and black sesame.
  • Daifuku: traditional filled mochi — red bean paste, fruit, custard, or cream — served unfrozen; anko-filled daifuku uses sweet red bean paste.
  • Popular flavors across mochi menus: strawberry, matcha, mango, chocolate, ube, black sesame, coconut, and red bean.
  • Mochi donuts: modern donut-style desserts with a chewy texture, often coated with glaze, chocolate, matcha, or ube.
  • Prices vary by location, type, and serving size — compare price per piece; menus may list per-piece, 4-piece, 6-piece box, assorted box, or gift-box prices.
