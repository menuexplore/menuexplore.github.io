L'Atelier de Joël Robuchon Menu & Prices: What to Order

L'Atelier de Joël Robuchon pairs contemporary French cuisine with the classic preparations of Joël Robuchon, served at counter-style seats facing the open kitchen. There is no single worldwide menu: each restaurant sets its own dishes and prices, and this guide covers the current Paris and Miami locations, keeping the former London and New York restaurants as history.

At Paris Étoile, on the Champs-Élysées, lunch menus are published at €52, €72 and €92, the dedicated vegetarian menu is €99 per person, and the Signature Menu is €220 per person, with dishes such as foie gras, caviar with cauliflower, scallops, quail or lamb, Robuchon-style pommes purée, and dessert with petits fours. Paris Saint-Germain, on Rue Montalembert, offers a Découverte tasting experience and à la carte dining; its current Découverte price was not confirmed from reliable published sources, so no figure is given here.

In the Miami Design District, the Seasonal tasting menu is $195 and the longer Evolution menu is $295, with lunch on selected days. During Miami Spice 2026 the restaurant also listed a $95 Chef's Experience Lunch and a $155 four-course dinner — promotional prices, not the standard year-round menu.

Signature dishes to look for include the famous pommes purée, caviar jelly with cauliflower cream, foie gras, quail with foie gras, scallops and lobster spaghetti. Menus and prices vary by location and season, so confirm the current menu with the restaurant before visiting.

Key facts & prices

  • Paris Étoile lunch menus: €52, €72 and €92
  • Paris Étoile Signature Menu: €220 per person
  • Paris Étoile vegetarian menu: €99 per person
  • Miami tasting menus: Seasonal $195, Evolution $295
  • Miami Spice 2026 promo: $95 Chef's Experience Lunch and $155 four-course dinner (promotional prices, not year-round)
