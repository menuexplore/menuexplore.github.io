Burnt Ends Menu Singapore: Prices, Dishes & Dining Guide

Burnt Ends is a live-fire barbecue restaurant at Dempsey Hill, Singapore, at 7 Dempsey Road, #01-04, Singapore 249671. Its menu centres on cooking over open fire: small snacks, seafood, grilled meats, vegetables and desserts, with many dishes intended for sharing. The exact selection is seasonal and can change, so a dish in an older review is not automatically on the current menu.

The restaurant publishes no fixed priced menu, so the figures in the guide come from dated third-party listings, reviews and diner reports. Reported prices run from about S$12–S$20 for small plates to S$95 for king crab with garlic sauce (2024 review). The best-known Burnt Ends Sanger — pulled pork shoulder in a bun with coleslaw and smoky chipotle aioli — was listed at around S$20, beef marmalade and pickles at S$14–S$16, roasted chicken at S$65–S$70, and beef tenderloin with burnt onion and bone marrow at S$114 for 285g. A 2023 report listed the bar’s “Classics” tasting menu at S$250 for two. Prices in Singapore may also have service charge and GST added, so confirm before visiting.

Lunch is served Thursday to Saturday, generally 12:00 PM to 2:30 PM, and dinner Tuesday to Saturday, approximately 6:00 PM to 11:00 PM. Burnt Ends is closed on Sunday and Monday. Seating matters: counter and bar seating may have a shorter à la carte selection or a tasting-style menu, while the main dining area offers the broader à la carte experience. Reservations are available through the restaurant’s official booking system. Guide last updated November 12, 2026.

Key facts

  • Burnt Ends Sanger — ~S$20 (Burpple listing, reported): pulled pork shoulder in a bun with coleslaw and smoky chipotle aioli.
  • King crab with garlic sauce — ~S$95 (2024 review, reported).
  • Beef marmalade and pickles — S$14–S$16 (reported); beef ssamjang ~S$20 (reported).
  • Roasted chicken S$65–S$70; beef tenderloin with burnt onion and bone marrow S$114 for 285g (diner reports).
