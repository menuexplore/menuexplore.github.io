Shukette Reservations 2026: Resy Tips, Walk-Ins & Best Times

Shukette, chef Ayesha Nurdjaja's Levantine restaurant at 230 9th Ave in Chelsea, New York, takes reservations through Resy — and for most people who want a particular table at a particular time, booking ahead is the safer plan. But Shukette is not reservations-only: part of its 23-seat concrete counter around the open kitchen is held for walk-ins, so showing up without a booking can work, especially for one or two people who are happy to sit at the counter and keep their timing flexible.

The hardest times to walk in are Friday and Saturday nights and the 7–9 PM window on any night. The best walk-in windows are the 5 PM weekday opening, after 9:30 PM once earlier reservations turn over, and Sundays, when the restaurant opens earlier at 4 PM and the night tends to be lighter than Saturday. Shukette serves dinner only — Monday to Saturday 5–11 PM and Sunday 4–10 PM — so there is no lunch service to use as an easier alternative.

Walk-ins are not realistic for larger parties: groups larger than four should book through Resy well ahead, ideally a week or more ahead for a specific date or time, and state the group size clearly when booking. The counter also puts you closest to the Al Ha'esh charcoal grill — a strength for a spontaneous pair, but not the right seat if the evening needs a conventional table together. Shukette's menu lists no prices online, so check the restaurant's current menu or ask your server for today's prices before you order. Guide last updated November 12, 2026.

Key facts

  • Reservations: through Resy; book a week or more ahead for a specific date or time, especially Friday and Saturday nights and parties larger than four.
  • Walk-ins: part of the 23-seat concrete counter around the open kitchen is held for walk-ins; expect a counter seat, not a standard table.
  • Best walk-in windows: the 5 PM weekday opening, after 9:30 PM, or Sunday (opens at 4 PM).
  • Hardest times: Friday and Saturday nights, and the 7–9 PM window on any night.
  • Hours and address: dinner only, Monday–Saturday 5–11 PM, Sunday 4–10 PM; 230 9th Ave, Chelsea, New York; phone (212) 242-1803. Prices: the menu lists no prices online — check the current menu or ask your server. Guide last updated November 12, 2026.
