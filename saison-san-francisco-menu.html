Saison San Francisco Menu & Prices: $368 Tasting Guide

Saison is a two-Michelin-star, live-fire New American restaurant in San Francisco's SoMa district, at 178 Townsend St, serving a single seasonal tasting menu — there is no a la carte menu. The restaurant earned three Michelin stars in 2014, a historic first for the city shared with Benu, but its own official site currently describes it as a two-Michelin-star restaurant. The dining room faces the open kitchen, so the best seats are the tables with a kitchen view, and reservations work more like concert tickets than restaurant bookings.

The tasting menu starts at $368 per person, and the OpenTable Experiences listing quotes a $368-$566 per-person range, suggesting the price can vary by date or experience type. That headline price is only the starting point: beverage pairings are sold separately, and a 20% service charge, sales tax and San Francisco's 6% mandate are all added on top. With a mid-range pairing, a $368 menu can land well north of $700 per person all-in, so treat that as a planning estimate and confirm the current figures on the official booking page before committing.

Because the menu is seasonal, there is no permanent dish list, but diners report roughly 15-22 courses, moving from light, raw and preserved preparations through seafood and vegetables into richer fire-cooked meats, closing with dessert. Dishes that keep appearing in diner accounts include the sea urchin liquid toast opening bite, the black truffle brioche croissant, caviar service, geoduck and uni toast, antelope with radicchio, A5 Miyazaki wagyu, five-spice duck, and the mandarin creamsicle dessert. There is also a lounge area where the full or an abbreviated menu can be enjoyed in a more relaxed setting.

Saison is open Tuesday-Saturday 5:00pm-9:30pm and closed Sunday and Monday; book online through saisonsf.com or by phone at (415) 828-7990 during office hours (10:00am-5:00pm). Reservations are final and nontransferable: cancellations and no-shows within 48 hours are charged the full menu price with no credit, while earlier cancellations cost $98 per person, which you get 30 days to apply as credit toward a rebooking. Check dietary limits before booking — vegan diets cannot be accommodated, and the kitchen cannot handle the complete exclusion of alliums, gluten, egg, vinegar, dairy, soy, finned fish, or mushrooms; email the restaurant with questions before reserving.

Prices and fees in this guide are based on the restaurant's official booking pages (via OpenTable Experiences) and verified third-party sources. Reported signature dishes come from diner accounts and may not be on the menu on your visit. Guide last updated November 12, 2026.
