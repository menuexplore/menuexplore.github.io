Ali's Chicken & Waffles Menu San Diego: Prices & Popular Picks 2026

Ali's Chicken & Waffles is a San Diego spot for buttermilk fried chicken and waffles, with two locations — 1139 Sixth Ave downtown and 4350 University Ave. Chicken-and-waffle combos run $10 to $18, all five chicken sandwiches are $8, and breakfast is served from 8 AM to 2 PM.

The menu is organized into several sections: a Most Ordered section, Chicken & Waffle Combos, Sandwiches, Soulcal Specials (tacos, quesadillas, loaded fries and burritos), Sides, A La Carte, Breakfast, Teas and Soft Drinks, and From the Cooler. The combo selection runs from the $10 Value Meal through Combo #1 at $13, Combo #2 at $15, Combo #4 at $15 with jumbo shrimp, and Combo #3 at $18. The five sandwiches — Original, Spicy, Home, Grilled and Chick-in-Waffle — are each listed at $8.

Breakfast runs from 8 AM to 2 PM and includes eggs, hash browns, waffles, French toast, pancakes, breakfast sandwiches, breakfast burritos, the Cluckfast Burrito, and shrimp and grits; breakfast items range from $3 to $15 and sides are $5.50. The restaurant's Most Ordered section lists Combo #2, the Spicy Chicken Sandwich, Combo #1, Ali's Burrito, Ali's Fries, the Cluckfast Burrito, Combo #3 and the Original Chicken Sandwich. Pickup and delivery are available, and the restaurant also promotes group ordering and catering. There is no separate lunch or dinner menu, and no dedicated vegetarian section — though items such as fries, mac and cheese, grits, waffles and pancakes do not list meat in their descriptions. Menu information checked September 30, 2026; prices and availability can change, so confirm the current total when placing an order.

Key facts

  • Chicken & waffle combos $10–$18 — Value Meal $10, Combo #1 $13, Combo #2 $15, Combo #4 $15 with jumbo shrimp, Combo #3 $18.
  • All five chicken sandwiches $8 each — Original, Spicy, Home, Grilled and Chick-in-Waffle.
  • Breakfast 8 AM–2 PM, $3–$15; sides $5.50.
  • Two San Diego locations: 1139 Sixth Ave, San Diego, CA 92101 (downtown) and 4350 University Ave, San Diego, CA 92105.
