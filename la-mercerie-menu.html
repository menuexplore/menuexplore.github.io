La Mercerie Menu & Prices: NYC French Café Guide 2026

La Mercerie is a contemporary French café tucked inside the Roman and Williams Guild design store at 53 Howard Street in SoHo, New York. It serves classic French cooking, a famous buckwheat crêpe, and a chocolate chip cookie that went viral on TikTok. The kitchen is led by chef partner Marie-Aude Rose, who cooks the everyday French food of her Parisian upbringing.

The restaurant publishes no per-dish prices on its official site. OpenTable lists $31–50 per person, and third-party reported dishes run about $25–$46: the signature buckwheat Crêpe Complète is reported at around $28, Boeuf Bourguignon around $45, Halibut Au Beurre Blanc around $46, French Toast around $25, and Profiteroles au chocolat around $17. Treat these as orientation, not a price list, and confirm before visiting.

Hours are Monday to Friday 11:30am–10pm, and Saturday and Sunday 10am–3pm and 5–10pm. Lunch runs Monday to Friday 11:30–2:30, dinner daily 5–10, and brunch Saturday and Sunday 10–2:30. Note the weekend gap: the kitchen closes 3–5pm between brunch and dinner. Reservations are via Resy and are strongly recommended, especially for weekend brunch. Guide last updated November 12, 2026.

Key facts

  • Crêpe Complète — ~$28 (reported, not official): buckwheat crêpe folded around ham, egg and cheese — the signature dish.
  • Boeuf Bourguignon — ~$45 (reported); Halibut Au Beurre Blanc — ~$46 (reported). Mains run about $42–$46.
  • French Toast — ~$25 (reported); Profiteroles au chocolat — ~$17 (reported); the viral chocolate chip cookie is a gooey, hazelnut-butter-enriched dessert.
  • OpenTable lists $31–50 per person. La Mercerie publishes no per-dish prices — every dish price here is third-party reported, never official.
  • 53 Howard St, New York, NY 10013 (SoHo, inside the Roman and Williams Guild). Phone: (212) 852-9097. Reservations via Resy; the weekend kitchen closes 3–5pm between brunch and dinner.
