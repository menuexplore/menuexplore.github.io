Marcel Atlanta Menu: Prices, Signature Steaks & What to Order (2026)

Marcel is Ford Fry's European-inspired steakhouse in Atlanta's Westside Provisions District, at 1170 Howell Mill Rd NW, Atlanta, GA 30318. The room leans 1920s glamour — dark, handsome, white-tablecloth energy without the stiffness — and the kitchen serves dinner only, opening at 5 PM. Marcel does not publish a full priced menu online, so this guide is honest about what can actually be verified: the signature dishes, the one confirmed price anchor, the realistic price band, the hours, and exactly how to plan the visit.

The one verified price is the $155-per-person "Le Boeuf Premier" event menu, listed in Marcel's official event materials from February 2026 — a prix-fixe-style event menu that sets the anchor: a full Marcel experience runs in the $150+ per person range before drinks. OpenTable lists Marcel at $50+ per person, and for a dinner of appetizer, steak, sides, dessert and a glass of wine, the guide's realistic budget is $100–$175 per person before tax and tip — an estimate based on the $155 anchor and the category, not a quoted price. Confirm specifics before visiting.

The dishes reviewers consistently name are the Filet Madame (dry-aged 8oz filet, the house signature steak), the 30oz dry-aged porterhouse for two, beef Wellington, lobster risotto, escargot, and the baked Alaska flamed tableside — the dessert order and the room's signature theater. Marcel is an OpenTable Icons pick for 2026 and 2025 and sits on Wine Enthusiast's 100 Best Wine Restaurants list. Reservations are via OpenTable, and prime weekend tables go fast, so book ahead for Fridays and Saturdays. Hours: Monday–Thursday and Sunday 5–10 PM, Friday–Saturday 5–11 PM. Guide last updated November 12, 2026.

Key facts

  • One verified price: the "Le Boeuf Premier" event menu — $155 per person (official event materials, February 2026).
  • Price band: OpenTable lists $50+ per person; a realistic full dinner is $100–$175 per person (guide's estimate, not a quoted price).
  • Address: 1170 Howell Mill Rd NW, Atlanta, GA 30318 — Westside Provisions District. Phone: +1 404-665-4555. Official site: marcelatl.com.
  • Hours: Mon–Thu & Sun 5–10 PM; Fri–Sat 5–11 PM — dinner only, no lunch service.
  • Signature dishes: Filet Madame (dry-aged 8oz filet), 30oz porterhouse for two, beef Wellington, lobster risotto, and baked Alaska flamed tableside.
