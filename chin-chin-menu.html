Chin Chin Menu & Prices 2026: Brentwood & Studio City

Chin Chin is a Chinese-American restaurant with two Los Angeles locations, in Brentwood and Studio City, known for dishes like Chinese Chicken Salad, General Tso Chicken and Peanut Noodles. The menu covers shareables, soups and salads, chicken, beef, seafood, noodles, rice, vegetables, desserts and beverages, plus weekday lunch bowls, a kids' menu, party packs and catering options.

Entrées run from $19 for chicken dishes to $25 for beef and $24–$28 for seafood. Prices from the current Los Angeles menu include General Tso Chicken at $19, Chinese Chicken Salad at $16 for a full serving and $12.95 for a half, Peanut Noodles at $15, Dan Dan Spicy Noodles at $18, Yang Chow Fried Rice at $17, Beef & Broccoli at $25 and Honey Walnut Shrimp at $28. Weekday lunch bowls run $12.95–$17.95, Monday to Friday, 11 a.m. to 3 p.m., with a choice of white or brown rice. Desserts include Chin Chin Churros at $8 and Cheesecake and Chocolate Torte at $9; Thai Iced Tea is $6 and iced teas are $4.

Both restaurants are open daily from 11 a.m. to 9 p.m. Brentwood is at 11740 San Vicente Blvd, Los Angeles, CA 90049, phone (310) 826-2525; Studio City is at 12215 Ventura Blvd, Studio City, CA 91604, phone (818) 985-9090. Both offer dine-in, takeout and delivery with online ordering through the official Chin Chin website. Prices can change and may vary by location or ordering channel, so the price shown when you select your restaurant should be treated as the final price. This is an independent guide and is not affiliated with or operated by Chin Chin. Guide last updated November 14, 2026.
