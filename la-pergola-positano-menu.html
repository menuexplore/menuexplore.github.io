La Pergola Positano Menu: Prices, Dishes & What to Order

La Pergola sits right on Spiaggia Grande in Positano, at Via del Brigantino 35/37, and its menu leans heavily on seafood and Italian cooking — raw seafood and crudi, pasta and risotto, grilled fish, meat dishes, Neapolitan-style pizza, salads, desserts, cocktails and wine. Brunch-style options such as eggs, omelettes, pancakes, crepes and pastries are also listed. The menu and prices in this guide were checked against the restaurant group’s digital menu on 30 September 2026; prices can change, and catch-of-the-day items may not have a fixed price.

Prices give a clear picture of what a meal costs. Pizza runs €19–€24, pasta and risotto €24–€32, fish mains €22–€35, meat dishes €24–€29 and desserts around €8–€16. A simple pizza plus a drink works out at roughly €35–€45 per person including the €4 cover charge, while a starter, pasta, dessert and drink can reach about €70–€90 per person. A minimum of one dish per person applies, and a €4 cover charge is added per person.

Standout dishes include spaghetti alle vongole (€30), fisherman’s risotto (€30), roasted octopus (€22), the Gran Crudo La Pergola raw-seafood selection (€120, best shared), and Margherita pizza (€20). Vegetarian diners have options such as eggplant parmigiana (€21), ricotta and provola ravioli (€24) and vegetarian pizzas. Opening hours were not independently verified for this guide, so check directly with the restaurant before visiting, especially outside the main tourist season.

Key facts

  • Location: Via del Brigantino 35/37, Spiaggia Grande, Positano — Italian, seafood and pizzeria.
  • Prices checked 30 September 2026; catch-of-the-day price varies — ask the restaurant for the current price.
  • Pizza €19–€24: Marinara €19, Margherita €20, Diavola €21, La Pergola €23.
  • Pasta & risotto €24–€32: spaghetti alle vongole €30, fisherman’s risotto €30, egg tagliolini with truffle €32.
  • Raw seafood: Gran Crudo La Pergola €120; oysters or raw prawns €8 each.
  • Fish mains €22–€35, meat dishes €24–€29; desserts around €8–€16; cover charge €4 per person, minimum one dish per person.
