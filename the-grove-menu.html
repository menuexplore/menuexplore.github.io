The Grove Menu & Prices: LA Restaurants, Dishes & Guide

The Grove in Los Angeles is a shopping and dining destination at 189 The Grove Drive, so there is no single Grove menu — every restaurant, café and counter has its own. This guide compares the venues by cuisine and price level, from the American Beauty steakhouse and Blue Ribbon Sushi to La Piazza and The Cheesecake Factory.

American Beauty is The Grove’s steakhouse, serving wood-grilled steaks, seafood, chicken, vegetables, sides and drinks across brunch, lunch and dinner. Alma Cocina de Mexico offers two experiences: a walk-up taqueria and market downstairs and a full-service dining room upstairs. Blue Ribbon Sushi Bar & Grill specialises in sushi, sashimi and Japanese dishes, with sake, wine and cocktails.

La Piazza and Sogno Toscano cover Italian food, while The Cheesecake Factory offers one of the broadest menus at The Grove — more than 200 items including pizza, pasta, salads, burgers, seafood and cheesecakes. Quick stops include Chill Since ’93, La La Land Cafe, % Arabica, Starbucks, Pressed, Häagen-Dazs, See’s Candies and Wetzel’s Pretzels. There is no single price range: a coffee, pretzel or ice cream costs far less than a steakhouse dinner. Prices, dishes and hours differ by venue, so use this guide for planning and check each restaurant’s current menu before visiting.

Key facts

  • The Grove is at 189 The Grove Drive, Los Angeles — a shopping and dining destination with no single menu; each restaurant has its own.
  • American Beauty’s dinner menu dated November 20, 2025 lists Creekstone Filet Mignon (7 oz) at $51 and Steakhouse Burger at $24 — historical examples, not guaranteed current prices.
  • The same November 20, 2025 menu lists Wood Grilled Flatbread $12, Crispy Brussels Sprouts $15, Mac & Cheese $14 and Mary’s ½ Chicken $29.
  • The Cheesecake Factory lists more than 200 menu items, including pizza, pasta, salads, burgers, seafood and cheesecakes.
  • Blue Ribbon Sushi and The Cheesecake Factory both list kids menus, and The Grove’s directory has a Family-Friendly Dining category.
