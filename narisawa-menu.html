Narisawa Tokyo Menu & Price 2026: The ¥68,000 Omakase, Explained

Narisawa is chef Yoshihiro Narisawa's omakase-only restaurant in Minami-Aoyama, Tokyo, built around his Innovative Satoyama Cuisine concept. There is no à la carte menu: guests receive the day's chef-selected tasting course, shaped by seasonal Japanese ingredients and what the kitchen can source at the time of the visit.

The official menu lists the omakase at ¥68,000 per person for both lunch and dinner, including tax and service. Booking platforms can show different totals, so the final amount is worth checking before confirming: OMAKASE lists a ¥78,200 all-inclusive course for November visits, and Tableall shows ¥89,500 including an ¥8,000 booking fee. Drinks and wine pairings are not included in the food-course price unless the reservation information specifically says so.

Signature dishes associated with the restaurant include Bread of the Forest, Soil Soup and Temari, but these are representative dishes rather than guaranteed items — the changing omakase means the dishes on your visit can be different. Vegetarian and vegan arrangements are available by advance request, and dietary restrictions or allergies should be shared during booking. Reservations are required, parties must be two or more guests, and guests must be 18 years or older. Guide last updated November 12, 2026.
