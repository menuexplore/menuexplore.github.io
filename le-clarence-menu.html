Le Clarence Menu & Prices 2026: Lunch, Dinner & Wine

Le Clarence is a two-Michelin-starred restaurant in a 19th-century private mansion near the Champs-Élysées in Paris. Led by Executive Chef Andrea Capasso, the kitchen builds its menus around seasonal products, and instead of conventional course names, the tasting menus are organised into “sequences”. There is no single fixed list of dishes: the exact preparations change with the seasons.

At lunch, guests can choose from three tasting formats — 3 sequences for €150, 4 sequences for €250, or the 6-sequence Inspiration Menu for €380 — or order from the seasonal lunch à la carte, available Tuesday to Friday. Dinner is centred on two tasting formats: the 4-sequence Le Clarence Menu at €280 per person and the 6-sequence Inspiration Menu at €430. Wine pairings are optional and priced separately, with Clarence pairings starting from €110 and Inspiration pairings from €390.

The autumn 2026 à la carte gives a snapshot of the kitchen’s current style, with starters from €65, mains from €95 and desserts at €35 each. Ordering several dishes à la carte can quickly cost as much as — or more than — a tasting menu, so the choice comes down to whether you want individual dish selection or a structured tasting experience. Prices were checked against the restaurant’s official information in October 2026 and may change.

Key facts & prices

  • 3-sequence lunch (Tuesday–Friday): €150 per person — the lowest-priced tasting format.
  • 4-sequence lunch: €250 per person; 6-sequence Inspiration lunch: €380 per person.
  • Dinner: 4 sequences €280 per person; 6-sequence Inspiration €430 per person.
  • Seasonal lunch à la carte: starters from €65, mains from €95, desserts €35 each (autumn 2026).
  • Wine pairings (optional, extra): Clarence from €110; Inspiration from €390. The cellar holds more than 1,500 references from over 500 winemakers.
