Babette Albenga Menu 2026: Ligurian Dishes & Tasting Menu Guide

Ristorante Babette is a seafood-focused Ligurian-Mediterranean restaurant at Via Michelangelo 17 in Albenga, Italy, close to the beach. The Michelin Guide lists it in its €€ price category. Its menu is built around fresh fish and shellfish and seasonal ingredients, alongside pasta, selected meat dishes and desserts — and the Michelin Guide highlights its crisp mixed fry of fish and vegetables as a notable preparation.

No itemized menu prices could be reliably verified for this guide, which was last updated November 12, 2026, so confirm current dishes and prices directly with the restaurant before visiting. Third-party listings give a sense of the cooking: marinated Ligurian anchovies with red onion confit and fennel, black tagliatelle with small cuttlefish, clams and broccoli, saffron ravioli with fresh catch and Albenga artichoke sauce, mixed grilled fish and shellfish, Fassona beef cheek with Nebbiolo sauce, and an almond and pear tartlet with salted caramel and praline ice cream. These are examples from available listings, not a guaranteed current menu.

The Michelin Guide describes a lighter lunch offering, listed 12:30 to 14:00, with dinner from 19:30 to 22:00 and Tuesday as the weekly closing day — confirm hours before travelling, especially off-season. A third-party listing also refers to a menu degustazione (tasting menu), but its courses, price and inclusions could not be verified. No dish in the sources is explicitly labelled vegetarian or gluten-free, so dietary requirements should be confirmed with the restaurant when booking.
