Chapter One Dublin Menu & Prices 2026: Lunch €95, Tasting €235

Chapter One by Mickael Viljanen is a set-menu fine-dining restaurant at 18-19 Parnell Square North, Dublin 1, in the basement of the Dublin Writers Museum. There is no traditional a la carte menu: guests choose between set lunch, dinner and tasting menus, and the menu information in this guide was compiled from the restaurant information supplied for the article. Seasonal dishes and prices may change, so confirm the latest details directly with Chapter One before booking.

Food starts at €95 per person for the three-course lunch, with a surprise lunch tasting menu at €150. The four-course dinner is €190 per person and the six-course dinner tasting menu is €235. An artisan cheese selection costs €29 when added to lunch. Matching wines are listed at €90 to €110 depending on the menu, and the sommelier selection ranges from €235 to €285. For two guests, the four-course dinner costs €380 for food before drinks or extras.

Sample dishes include foie gras royale with apple, walnut and smoked eel, Clare Island organic salmon, Castletownbere cod, Kari Gosse glazed Donegal lobster, roast Anjou pigeon, young Wicklow sika deer and Valrhona chocolate desserts. The listed opening hours are Tuesday to Friday, 12:30pm-2:00pm and 5:30pm-10:00pm, with Saturday dinner only; the restaurant is closed Sunday and Monday. These are third-party hours, so confirm service times directly with the restaurant. The Michelin Guide lists a vegetarian menu, but the restaurant states it cannot cater for vegan diets or egg or dairy intolerance. Reservations are made through the restaurant's official website. Note that another restaurant named Chapter One operates in Kent, England - check the Dublin address when booking.
