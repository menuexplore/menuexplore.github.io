Minetta Tavern Menu & Prices: The $38 Black Label Burger

Minetta Tavern is Keith McNally's French-American steakhouse at 113 MacDougal Street in Greenwich Village, New York, and it is one of the hardest tables in the Village to book. The restaurant is famous for one dish above all: the $38 Black Label Burger, a thick patty of prime dry-aged beef cuts topped with caramelized onions and served with pommes frites. Burgers run $31-$38, entrees $35-$45 and steaks $47-$189, topped by the Dry-Aged Cote de Boeuf at $189 for two.

The menu covers the full French-American steakhouse range. The grillades section runs from Steak Frites at $47-$49 to the Bone-In New York Strip at $75-$77 and the Filet Mignon Sauce au Poivre at $58-$59. Entrees include Smoked Duck Breast at $45, Squab en Croute at $43 and Truffled Roasted Half Chicken at $41. Starters feature the signature Roasted Bone Marrow at $34 and Foie Gras Terrine at $35, while sides such as frites, aligot and sauteed spinach run $14-$18.

Desserts run $15-$26, led by the Souffle for Two at $26 in Grand Marnier or chocolate — made to order, so order it early in the meal. Lunch runs Wednesday to Friday, 12:00pm-3:30pm, and weekend brunch runs Saturday to Sunday, 11:00am-3:30pm, with dishes such as the Lobster Roll at $35 and Eggs Benedict at $29. Dinner is daily 5:00pm-11:00pm with a late supper service until midnight. Reservations are strongly recommended; walk-ins are occasionally possible at the bar, particularly early in dinner service or at supper. A typical dinner for two runs $150-$250 before drinks, tax and tip.

Prices are from the guide's current listed menus corroborated against delivery listings; where sources disagree the guide shows a range, so confirm the latest menu before visiting. Guide last updated November 12, 2026.

Key facts

  • Black Label Burger: $38 — prime dry-aged beef, caramelized onions, pommes frites; Minetta Burger: $31-$33.
  • Dry-Aged Cote de Boeuf: $189 for two (about $95 per person before sides, drinks, tax and tip); steaks $47-$189.
  • Souffle for Two: $26 (Grand Marnier or chocolate), made to order — order it early.
  • Entrees $35-$45, desserts $15-$26; typical dinner for two $150-$250 before drinks, tax and tip.
  • Hours: lunch Wed-Fri 12:00pm-3:30pm; dinner daily 5:00pm-11:00pm; supper daily 11:00pm-12:00am; brunch Sat-Sun 11:00am-3:30pm. Reservations strongly recommended.
