Kimball House Menu 2026: Decatur Oysters, Happy Hour & Prices

Kimball House is an oyster house and cocktail bar inside a historic 1891 train depot at 303 E Howard Ave in Decatur, Georgia. It pairs a serious raw bar — a rotating list of 16 to 25 oyster varieties — with a James Beard-nominated cocktail program and a seasonal New American dinner menu. It is dinner-only, closed on Mondays, and one of the most-booked date-night tables in metro Atlanta.

Prices in this guide are diner-reported, because the restaurant publishes its menus as images on its official site rather than as a text price list, and the guide was last updated November 12, 2026. Starters run around $16: blue crab claws cost $16 and five grilled oysters cost $16. The best-known deal is the weekday oyster happy hour, reportedly about $1.50 per oyster from 5–7pm, based on 2025 coverage — confirm the current timing and pricing before visiting, since the restaurant is now closed on Mondays.

Reviewers consistently name the same dishes: raw oysters on the half shell, the Hawaiian rolls with pineapple jam, cavatelli with rock shrimp, beef tartare, and vanilla crème brûlée for dessert, plus a French 75 or a Sazerac from the acclaimed bar. Mains include roasted duck breast, Kimball House steak, ribeye, cod cheeks, Spanish octopus and whole roasted lobster. Hours are Tuesday–Thursday and Sunday 5–10pm, Friday–Saturday 5–11pm, closed Monday. Reservations are recommended through the website (Resy) or by phone at (404) 378-3502, and a 4% employee wellness fee is added to all checks.

Key facts

  • Location: 303 E Howard Ave, Decatur, GA 30030 — inside a historic 1891 train depot; phone (404) 378-3502.
  • Raw bar: 16–25 rotating oyster varieties on the half shell; weekday oyster happy hour reportedly ~$1.50 per oyster, 5–7pm (2025 coverage — confirm before visiting).
  • Blue crab claws $16; grilled oysters (5) $16 — diner-reported prices.
  • Must-orders (diner-reported): Hawaiian rolls with pineapple jam, cavatelli with rock shrimp, beef tartare, vanilla crème brûlée; cocktails French 75 and Sazerac.
  • Hours: Tue–Thu 5–10pm, Fri–Sat 5–11pm, Sun 5–10pm, Mon closed; 4% employee wellness fee on all checks. Guide last updated November 12, 2026.
