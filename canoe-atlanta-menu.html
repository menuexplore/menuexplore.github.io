Canoe Atlanta Menu & Prices 2026: Riverside Fine Dining in Vinings

Canoe is a modern American fine-dining restaurant on the Chattahoochee River in Atlanta's historic Vinings neighborhood, known for its riverside patio, slow-braised rabbit and peppercorn-crusted kangaroo loin. It has collected awards for decades — a Mobil 4 Star, a James Beard "Best New Restaurants" nod, induction into the Nation's Restaurant News Fine Dining Hall of Fame, and OpenTable Icon status in both 2025 and 2026 — and the OpenTable crowd still rates it 4.7 stars across roughly 12,000 reviews.

The food is elegant modern American under executive chef Matthew Basford: slow-braised Blue Ridge rabbit, maple-brined duck breast and a peppercorn-crusted kangaroo loin that surprises everyone. Dinner entrees run $28–$69, appetizers $13–$22, and every dessert — from pastry chef Jessica Champagne's kitchen — is $14. Lunch runs $10–$33 and Sunday brunch $3–$32. Expect $31–$50+ per person before wine, and note that some diners report $300+ for a party of four once drinks enter the picture.

The dishes that define Canoe are the Peppercorn Crusted Kangaroo Loin ($22), the Slow Braised Blue Ridge Rabbit ($40), the Maple Brined Duck Breast ($52), the Oven Roasted Australian Lamb Top Sirloin ($49) and the Hickory Grilled Bay of Fundy Salmon ($42) — and Lolly's Pecan Pie ($14) is the dessert to beat. Dinner runs Monday–Thursday and Sunday 5:30–9:30pm and Friday–Saturday 5:30–10pm; lunch is Monday–Friday 11:30am–2pm and brunch is Sunday 10:30am–2:30pm. Reservations are essential on weekends, the dress code is business casual, valet parking is complimentary, and there is no kids' menu.

All prices come straight from Canoe's official menu (canoeatl.com/menu, checked October 2026); seasonal dishes rotate, so confirm before visiting. Guide last updated November 12, 2026.
