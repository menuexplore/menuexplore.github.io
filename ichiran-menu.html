Ichiran Menu: Tonkotsu Ramen Prices, Toppings & Solo Booths (NYC 2026)

Ichiran is the Japanese Hakata-style tonkotsu ramen chain famous for its solo "Ramen Focus Booths" — private counter seats with dividers and a bamboo blind, where your bowl is served through the blind and the focus is entirely on the ramen. In New York City there are four locations: Times Square (152 W 49th St), Midtown / Herald Square (132 W 31st St), and two in Brooklyn (including 374 Johnson Ave).

The menu is tiny by design: essentially one ramen plus a veggie version, with everything else customization and add-ons. The Classic Tonkotsu Ramen costs $20–$22 — the menu lists $20, while recent diners report $22 at Times Square. The Veggie Ramen, a dashi-based bowl, is $20. Every bowl comes with Ichiran's original Spicy Red Sauce, and the order form lets you set dashi (saltiness), richness, spice level 1–10, noodle texture, garlic, and scallion/chashu. First-timers are advised medium dashi, medium richness, spice level 2, firm noodles and 1 clove of garlic.

Add-ons run $2–$11: the essential kaedama noodle refill is $4, a soft-boiled egg $3, extra chashu $4.50, and premium yakibuta $11. Drinks are $3–$4.50, and the take-home ramen kit is $29. A first-timer meal — bowl, kaedama and matcha pudding — comes to roughly $30–$32 before tax and tip. Expect a line at Times Square and Midtown, especially evenings and weekends; off-peak lunch hours have the shortest wait.

All prices here are from Ichiran's current NYC menu listings (checked October 2026), guide last updated November 12, 2026. Menus change, so confirm before visiting.

Key facts

  • Classic Tonkotsu Ramen $20–$22 (menu lists $20; $22 reported at Times Square); Veggie Ramen $20 — current NYC menu listings checked October 2026.
  • Kaedama noodle refill $4; add-ons $2–$11 (soft-boiled egg $3, extra chashu $4.50, premium yakibuta $11); take-home ramen kit $29.
  • First-timer meal ~$30–$32 (bowl + kaedama + matcha pudding); full ritual ~$40; vegetarian (veggie bowl + kaedama) ~$24.
  • Solo "Ramen Focus Booths" with dividers and bamboo blind; order form sets dashi, richness, spice 1–10, noodle texture, garlic, scallion/chashu — first-timers: medium/medium/spice 2/firm/1 clove.
  • 4 NYC locations: Times Square 152 W 49th St, Midtown 132 W 31st St, two in Brooklyn (incl. 374 Johnson Ave); official site ichiranusa.com; the same bowl costs ¥980 (~$6–$7) in Japan.
