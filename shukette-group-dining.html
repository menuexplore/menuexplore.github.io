Shukette Group Dining 2026: Ordering Guide for 4, 6 & 8+ People

Planning Shukette group dining in Chelsea? Chef Ayesha Nurdjaja's Levantine restaurant at 230 9th Avenue runs on one rule: two to three shared dishes per person, spread across breads, dips, Shuk vegetable plates and the charcoal grill. There is no appetizer-then-entree path here — the whole table eats everything together, which is why birthdays land here so often, and why groups either order beautifully or end up with a table of dips and no dinner.

A balanced order touches every part of the menu: house-baked breads like Frena and Lafa, two or three dips such as Not Your Average Hummus, Labneh and Beet Tzatziki, Shuk plates like Pea Party, Glazed Sweet Potatoes and Jeweled Rice, at least one or two charcoal-grilled dishes — Joojeh Chicken, Fish in a Cage or the Grass-Fed Steak Shish Kebab — and The Mic Drop, the tahini soft serve, to finish. For four people that is roughly 1 bread, 2 dips, 2 Shuk plates, 1 grilled main and 1 dessert; for six, about 15 shared dishes; for eight or more, order in waves — breads and dips first, Shuk plates next, then the grill.

Book through Resy, well ahead, and state your group size clearly: the room is a 23-seat concrete counter around the open kitchen plus standard tables, and a big party cannot be improvised at the last minute. Part of the counter is held for walk-ins, but that is not realistic for a larger group, and the restaurant's own materials describe no private-events route — no packages, room hires or set group menus. Flag dietary needs when you book: the Shuk section is vegetable-led and the Chickpea Flaxseed Cracker is the named gluten-free bread option. Shukette serves dinner only, Monday to Saturday 5–11pm and Sunday 4–10pm; phone (212) 242-1803. Shukette publishes no prices online, so check the restaurant's current menu or ask your server for today's prices. Guide last updated November 12, 2026.
