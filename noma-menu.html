Noma Menu & Prices 2026: Tasting Menu Cost in Copenhagen

Noma is Copenhagen's New Nordic restaurant on the Refshalevej waterfront. It closed in January 2026 and reopened in August 2026 as a new chapter under Executive Head Chef Pablo Soto, with Mette Brink Soberg as Head of R&D and Annika de Las Heras as CEO; founder Rene Redzepi moved into a creative director role. The concept is "Twelve Seasons": every month gets its own expression, so the menu reinvents itself month to month — sometimes week to week — and there is no fixed menu and no a la carte.

The price is simple: one set tasting menu at 4,500 DKK (about $686) per person, with an optional wine pairing at 2,000 DKK (about $305) — roughly $991 with the pairing. These are the set-menu and pairing prices reported at the August 2026 reopening. There is no cheaper lunch menu and no published drinks pricing beyond the pairing.

Booking is only through the official site, noma.dk — there are no walk-ins, and releases sell out extremely fast; the first three months after the reopening were reported nearly fully booked shortly after release. Service is Wednesday to Saturday, dinner, and smart casual is the safe dress code. Noma was named World's Best Restaurant five times and previously held three Michelin stars (awarded 2021), but it is not listed in the 2026 Michelin Guide, having not been open long enough in 2026. If you cannot get a table, Noma Projects — the restaurant's product line from its R&D team — is the official way to taste the kitchen's work without a reservation.

All prices are the verified set-menu and pairing prices reported at the August 2026 reopening. Dishes mentioned are examples from recent published reviews, clearly labeled — they are not a current menu. Guide last updated November 14, 2026.
