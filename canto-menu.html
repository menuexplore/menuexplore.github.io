Canto Menu Lower Parel, Mumbai: Prices, Popular Dishes & Cost for Two

Canto Restaurant & Banquet is a multi-cuisine restaurant at Level P5, Club House, Peninsula Business Park, Ganapatrao Kadam Marg, Lower Parel, Mumbai – 400013. It serves Italian, Indian, Asian and Japanese dishes, from pizza and pasta to biryani and sushi, along with Continental, Mexican, American, North Indian, Lebanese and Pan-Asian dishes and desserts.

Prices in this guide are based on a third-party menu listing checked on October 3, 2026. Light dishes start at around ₹120, most mains fall in the ₹300–₹500 range, and the estimated cost for two is ₹1,700–₹2,500 according to third-party listings. Prices can change, so confirm the current menu with the restaurant before visiting.

Pizzas are 10 inches, with vegetarian pizzas at ₹350–₹400 and non-vegetarian at ₹430–₹450. Pasta and risotto run ₹350–₹450. Indian mains range ₹260–₹500, biryani ₹350–₹430, sushi ₹450–₹500, and desserts ₹210–₹250. Vegetarian choices appear across most sections, from hummus and mezze to vegetarian pizzas, pasta, Indian curries, wraps, baos and sushi. The restaurant lists a bar, indoor and outdoor dining and wheelchair accessibility, opens around 12 noon, and can be reached at +91 96197 12200. Guide last updated November 12, 2026.
