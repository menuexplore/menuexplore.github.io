Rubirosa Menu: Prices for Pizza, Pasta & the Famous Tie-Dye Pie (NYC)

Rubirosa is a family-style Italian restaurant and pizzeria at 235 Mulberry St in Nolita, New York, between Prince and Spring Streets. It has been packing its little Mulberry Street dining room for years on the strength of paper-thin, blistered-crust pizza and handmade pastas — and you almost certainly need a reservation. The order that made it famous is the Tie-Dye pizza: tomato sauce, vodka sauce and basil pesto swirled together over fresh mozzarella on the signature thin crust, $33 for a small.

Pizzas come in Small and Large, plus an extensive gluten-free lineup. Small pizzas run $29–$33, large $38–$41 and gluten-free $34–$37 on the current delivery menu. The small menu lists the Classic, Fresca, Bianca, Arugula and Cacio e Pepe at $29 each, Vodka at $31, Honey Pie at $32, and the Tie-Dye, Rubirosa Supreme and Sausage & Broccoli Rabe at $33 each. Large pies are $38–$41, and the gluten-free Tie-Dye is $37. The pasta program is handmade daily: Spaghetti alla Chitarra $26, Lumache alla Vodka $27, Ricotta Ravioli $27, Cavatelli $29, Rubirosa Ragu $30 and Spaghetti & Meatballs $34, plus the Sunday Sauce at $56 and the Family Style Lasagne at $67, built for sharing.

Starters run $14–$23, including broccoli rabe ($14), garlic knots ($15), mozzarella sticks ($16) and meatballs ($23); salads are $20–$24 and soups $16. The secondi include Eggplant Parmigiano ($34), Chicken Parmigiano ($36) and Chicken Al Limone ($41). Rubirosa also runs a full gluten-free pizza and pasta menu with a dedicated fryer — a genuinely better setup than most NYC Italian restaurants offer. Reservations are strongly recommended via Resy, prime-time tables go fast, and there is a daily Pizza Hour from 4–5 PM. One note: rubirosa.com is not the restaurant — it is an unrelated Italian shoe brand. The restaurant’s official site is rubirosanyc.com.

All prices here are from Rubirosa’s current delivery menu (checked October 2026), guide last updated November 12, 2026. Dine-in prices may vary slightly, and menus change, so confirm before visiting.
