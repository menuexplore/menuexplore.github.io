L'Ambroisie Menu 2026: Prices, Dishes & Tasting Menu

L'Ambroisie is a two-Michelin-star French haute-cuisine restaurant at 9 Place des Vosges in Paris, now led by chef Shintaro Awa after Bernard Pacaud's departure in August 2025. Its eight-step seasonal tasting menu costs €410 per person for the whole table, while à la carte dishes run €45–€190, with prices net and service included. The menu follows the seasons, so dishes and prices can change.

The tasting menu moves through langoustine, purple artichoke, Kristal caviar, Dover sole, pigeon, cheese, grape and peach. Highlights include the Langoustine Royale opener, Kristal caviar with potato and celery, Dover sole in broth with chanterelles, and a final peach course with vineyard peach, candied blackcurrant and Greek yogurt infused with marigold. The cheese course features fresh and mature cheeses from Maison Alléosse.

À la carte, signature dishes include the langoustine feuillantine with sesame seeds and curry sauce (€145), sea bass with sliced artichoke and Kristal caviar (€190), lobster navarin with rosemary (€140) and the bitter chocolate tart with Bourbon vanilla ice cream (€48). Two people would pay €820 for the eight-step tasting menu. The restaurant serves dinner on Tuesday and lunch and dinner Wednesday to Saturday, and is closed on Sunday and Monday. Lunch starts at 12:15 pm and dinner at 8:00 pm. Menu and prices last checked: October 2026.

Key facts

  • Eight-step seasonal tasting menu: €410 per person, for the whole table
  • À la carte prices: €45–€190, net with service included
  • Sea bass with artichoke and Kristal caviar: €190; langoustine feuillantine: €145; lobster navarin: €140
