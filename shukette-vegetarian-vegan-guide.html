Shukette Vegetarian & Vegan Guide 2026: Best Meat-Free Dishes

Shukette, chef Ayesha Nurdjaja's Levantine restaurant in Chelsea, New York, is one of the city's easiest vegetarian tables. The Shuk section of the menu was built around seasonal vegetables, breads and dips from the start, not adapted as a dietary workaround. Vegans can eat very well here too, with a quick check with your server, because a few of the most famous dips are yogurt-based.

The reliable vegetarian backbone of a Shukette meal is the house breads, Frena, a Moroccan-style loaf finished with garlic and oregano, and Lafa, a grilled flatbread brushed with ramp butter, plus Not Your Average Hummus topped with marinated chickpeas, Spring Pea Dip with fresh spring garlic, preserved lemon and mint, Pea Party, Glazed Sweet Potatoes, Baby Artichokes a la Plancha and Jeweled Rice with saffron, cranberry, dill, cilantro and pistachio. Order a spread across breads, dips and Shuk dishes rather than one entree.

For vegans, the Spring Pea Dip is the most dependable vegan dip on the list, and Not Your Average Hummus is the other natural anchor. Labneh and Beet Tzatziki are yogurt-based, so they are vegetarian but not vegan, and Glazed Sweet Potatoes arrive with charred scallion creme fraiche while Lafa carries ramp butter, so flag your requirements when you sit down. The Chickpea Flaxseed Cracker is the only item the menu explicitly calls out as gluten-free.

Shukette is at 230 9th Ave, Chelsea, New York. It serves dinner only, Monday to Saturday 5-11pm and Sunday 4-10pm. Book tables through Resy; part of the 23-seat counter around the open kitchen is held for walk-ins. The menu lists no prices online, so ask for today's prices before you order. Guide last updated November 12, 2026.

Key facts

  • The Shuk is the vegetable-focused heart of the menu; meat-free plates are the main event, not a workaround.
  • Most dependable vegan dip: Spring Pea Dip (spring garlic, preserved lemon, mint); Not Your Average Hummus is the other vegan anchor.
  • Not vegan: Labneh and Beet Tzatziki are yogurt-based; Glazed Sweet Potatoes come with creme fraiche.
  • Only named gluten-free option: Chickpea Flaxseed Cracker.
