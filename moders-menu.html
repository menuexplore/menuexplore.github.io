Moders Menu: Prices, Popular Dishes & What to Order

Moders Oll Hus is a casual fish and seafood restaurant in Norden, Germany, in the Norddeich area of Lower Saxony. Its menu is reported to centre on fish and seafood — redfish, whiting, plaice, cod, salmon and matjes — with mussels, shrimp and crab soup also mentioned in third-party listings and guest reports. For diners who do not want fish, the reported alternatives are schnitzel, currywurst and salads.

No current official Moders Oll Hus website or official online price list could be verified, so every price in this guide is reported or unverified rather than confirmed. A September 2026 guest review reported a total of €132 for five people, including one starter, five main courses and five drinks, while an older review reported approximately €40 for two meals, two drinks and a dessert. Restaurant listings place the restaurant around €11–20 per person, with individual guest reports mentioning totals in the €10–20 and €20–30 ranges depending on what was ordered.

Among the reported dishes, the plaice fillet with bacon beans and grandmother-style fried potatoes (Schollenfilet mit Speckbohnen und Omas Bratkartoffeln) was reported at €16.90 in a guest review, though that figure may be outdated. Redfish and whiting fillets are reported served with fried potatoes, and a mussel-and-shrimp pan and potatoes with mussels are listed among the seafood options. Desserts reported include Rote Grütze and chocolate pudding with vanilla sauce, and beer was reported at €4 by an aggregator, not verified as current. The restaurant is listed at Deichstr. 9, 26506 Norden, and its phone number is +49 4931 8703. Online sources disagree about opening hours, so call before travelling rather than relying on a listing.

Key facts

  • Restaurant: Moders Oll Hus, Deichstr. 9, 26506 Norden, Lower Saxony, Germany — phone +49 4931 8703.
  • Cuisine: Fish and seafood first (redfish, whiting, plaice, cod, salmon, matjes, mussels, shrimp, crab soup), plus schnitzel, currywurst and salads.
  • Plaice fillet: Schollenfilet mit Speckbohnen und Omas Bratkartoffeln reported at €16.90 in a guest review; may be outdated — not verified as current.
  • Guest spending: €132 for five people reported in a September 2026 guest review; listings suggest roughly €11–20 per person. All figures are reported, not confirmed current prices.
  • Beer: €4 reported by an aggregator, not verified as current. No official price list could be verified.
