Joe Beef Menu Montreal 2026: Lobster Spaghetti, Prices & What to Order

Joe Beef is Montreal's famous French-Canadian tavern in Little Burgundy (2491 Notre-Dame Street West), known for lobster spaghetti and filet mignon with bone marrow. It publishes no fixed priced menu — the menu changes seasonally — and a full dinner runs roughly $100–150 per person.

There is no fixed Joe Beef menu to check before you go: the menu is seasonal and ever-changing, and that is the concept, not a flaw. What you can know in advance is what the kitchen does best, what dinner will likely cost, and how to get a table. Joe Beef has been serving rich French-Canadian cooking in a lively, candlelit tavern since 2005, holds a Michelin Plate, and books weeks out.

The dishes that define the restaurant are the lobster spaghetti (Spaghettis au homard) — the signature, and the one dish to order if you order only one — and the filet mignon with bone marrow. The meal often starts at the raw bar with fresh oysters and seafood platters, followed by starters such as foie gras, bone-marrow beef tartare, and Burgundy escargot. Steak frites and hangar steak cover the bistro classics, and the beef-fat fries (Île d'Orléans potatoes, garlic butter, parmesan) were singled out among Montreal's best fry addresses in 2026.

Reservations are essential, especially on weekends — book two to three weeks ahead for Friday and Saturday. Joe Beef is open Tuesday–Saturday, 5:00 PM–10:30 PM, and closed Sunday and Monday. The dress code is smart casual, and the dining room is lively and candlelit — convivial, not hushed.

Key facts

  • No fixed priced menu — the menu is seasonal and ever-changing; full dinner roughly $100–150 per person, OpenTable lists CAN$51 and over.
  • Signature dishes: lobster spaghetti (Spaghettis au homard) and filet mignon with bone marrow; also steak frites, fresh oysters, foie gras, and bone-marrow beef tartare.
  • Location: 2491 Notre-Dame Street West, Montreal, QC H3J 1N6 — Little Burgundy, near Atwater Market. Phone: (514) 935-6504.
  • Hours: Tuesday–Saturday, 5:00 PM–10:30 PM. Closed Sunday and Monday. Dress code: smart casual.
