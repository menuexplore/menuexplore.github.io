Selma, AL Restaurant Menus & Prices 2026: Where to Eat in Selma

Selma is a small city in Alabama, and this guide pulls its scattered restaurant menus into one place. It covers five restaurants: The Sandbar, Tally-Ho, Lannie’s Bar-B-Q Spot, Sam’s Southern Eatery and Mi Tenampa. Together they span BBQ and soul food, seafood, Mexican, Southern plates and casual American food, so you can compare menus by cuisine or by meal — breakfast, lunch or dinner — before you decide where to eat.

The Sandbar sits at the Selma City Marina on the Alabama River at 1 Marina Dr, with lunch, dinner and a Sunday brunch from 11 a.m. to 3 p.m. Its lunch menu lists appetizers at about $6–$14, including cheese balls ($10), wings ($14) and fried pickles ($8), salads at $8–$14, and a burger at $7. Tally-Ho, at 509 Mangum Ave, is a dinner restaurant known for steaks and seafood, with appetizers around $8–$13, a house salad at $4 small or $6 large, and sides such as stone-ground cheese grits ($4) and redskin mashed potatoes ($5).

Sam’s Southern Eatery at 307 Highland Ave focuses on fried fish, shrimp, po’boys, burgers and wings. A November 2025 third-party listing showed fish platters at about $16–$19, 6 whole wings at $15.49 and family portions from $39.49. Lannie’s Bar-B-Q Spot at 2115 Minter Ave serves BBQ and soul food — pulled pork sandwiches, rib sandwiches, swamp burgers and corn nuggets — but its current prices were not verified for the guide, so confirm them with the restaurant. Mi Tenampa at 1629 W Highland Ave is Selma’s Mexican option, with tacos al carbón, al pastor tacos, fajitas, nachos and chimichangas, but third-party listings disagreed on its prices, so check its current menu or call before ordering.

All prices here are sample or reference figures from the guide, last checked September 30, 2026 and last updated November 12, 2026. Menus and prices change, and delivery apps can show different prices from a restaurant’s own menu, so confirm the current total with the restaurant before ordering.
