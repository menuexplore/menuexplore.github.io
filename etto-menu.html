Etto Menu DC & Prices 2026: Pizza, Small Plates & Wine

Etto is a wood-fired pizza and Italian small-plates restaurant at 1541 14th Street NW in Washington, DC's Logan Circle, serving its pizza-and-wine-bar concept since 2013. Its compact menu is built around seven core wood-fired pizzas, seasonal pies that rotate through the year, and shareable small plates, cheeses, cured meats, seafood and desserts, alongside Italian wines, cocktails and spirits. The core pizzas — Margherita, Marinara, Salami, Americano, Alla Romana, Sicilian Eggplant and Prosciutto & Arugula — appeared unchanged on both the May 29, 2024 and November 17, 2024 menus, while seasonal pizzas, vegetables, cocktails and board items change regularly.

On the November 2024 menu, core pizzas ran $13–$21 and seasonal pizzas $23–$24; checked against the official menu dated November 13, 2026, the core pizzas are still $13–$21 and the seasonal pizzas are $23 each. Small plates run $12–$17, salads $13, cheese and cured meats $14–$20, house cocktails $15 and desserts $9–$12 on the dated menu. A 20% service charge is added to dine-in checks (and a 20% pre-tax service fee applies to to-go orders), and it is not a tip. All prices here are dated reference prices from the menus reviewed — the guide was last updated November 14, 2026 — so check Etto's current menu before ordering.

Key facts

  • Wood-fired pizza restaurant at 1541 14th Street NW, Logan Circle, Washington, DC — serving since 2013.
  • Core pizzas $13–$21 (Margherita $19, Marinara $13); seasonal pizzas $23 each on the official menu checked November 13, 2026.
  • Small plates $12–$17, salads $13 each, cheese & cured meats $14–$20, house cocktails $15, desserts $9–$12 (November 2024 menu, dated reference).
  • 20% service charge added to dine-in checks — not a tip; 20% pre-tax service fee for to-go orders.
  • Takeout: Tuesday–Friday 5:00–8:30 p.m., Saturday–Sunday 4:00–8:30 p.m., Monday closed (dated operating details — confirm before visiting).
